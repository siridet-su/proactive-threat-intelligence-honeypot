# Typed cross-family relationship decision

Decision date: 2026-07-30

Baseline: `c5554e9`

Disposition: **retain relationships as contextual provenance; do not activate
cross-family hypotheses or guidance**

## Evidence boundary

The typed layer may connect facts through an exact shared entity identity and
record observed event order. That supports statements such as "these observed
commands referenced the same normalized path." It does not prove that:

- a command transfer produced bytes at that path;
- a decode command produced the later execution target;
- an archive contains the searched data;
- a later network command transferred that archive;
- any command completed its claimed effect; or
- the sequence expresses attacker intent.

Transfer commands are attempts, transformations and archives are shadow-only,
and Cowrie compound outcomes are not fragment proof. Reordering, failure,
missing events, and repeated identities prevent causal interpretation even
when a syntactic relationship is resolvable.

## Decision

The minimum PoC displays the independently grounded family findings and their
shared evidence/identity provenance. It emits no transfer→transform→execution
or search→archive→transfer hypothesis, specialized cross-family guidance, or
causal chain status.

Activation is deferred until an evidence source can prove the relevant
artifact identity and per-stage outcome. Adding a score, Bayesian layer,
knowledge graph, ML model, or LLM prose would not create that missing
observation and would increase overclaim risk.

The frozen evaluation and holdout require complete relationship/chain
reference resolution, deterministic reconstruction, prediction/enrichment
isolation, and only the already activated independent-family outputs.
